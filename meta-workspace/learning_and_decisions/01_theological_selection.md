# Decision Record 01: Theological Selection & Scriptural Architecture

**Status:** Approved & Active  
**Context:** Alternate Universe Engine (`SUBVERTED_HARRY`)  
**Scope:** Design rationale behind *The Covenant of the Vessel* and its canonical architecture.

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

In standard Harry Potter canon, the Dursleys' mistreatment of Harry is depicted in a fairytale-like, cartoonish manner (locking him in a cupboard, screaming, casual physical abuse). In a narrative that aims for psychological realism, systemic warfare, and serious worldbuilding, this dynamic fails to explain:
1. Why Harry doesn't simply report them to authorities or fight back early.
2. How the household maintained moral justification without collapsing under external social scrutiny.
3. The psychological origin of Harry's later adult defense mechanisms (Lockhart-style bravado masking imposter syndrome and extreme neglect).

## 2. Decision: The Covenant of the Vessel

We replaced cartoonish villainy with a **historically grounded, ascetic, high-demand religious tradition**:
* **The Root Doctrine:** An ancient faith based on the metaphysics of **The Vessel** (form, containment, boundary) versus **The Leach / The Seep** (formlessness, decay, unearned phenomena).
* **The Psychological Mechanism:** The Dursleys do not view themselves as cruel abusers; they view themselves as righteous stewards performing severe spiritual rescue (*"Corrective Friction"*).
* **Petunia's Silence:** Petunia practices *willful blindness*—refusing to look at or acknowledge Harry because doing so acknowledges Lily's "contamination" and threatens the spiritual standing of Number Four.

## 3. Structural Decision: The Ancient Scripture Architecture

Rather than inventing modern fantasy rituals, the religion's scripture is modeled on **ancient Near Eastern suzerainty treaties and classical purity codes** (Levitical law, Zoroastrian *Vendidad*, Jain asceticism):
* The scripture is ancient, poetic, and uncompromising.
* The modern practice in 1980s/1990s Little Whinging is an application of this ancient diagnostic to modern life.
